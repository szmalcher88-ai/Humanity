Volume VIII — The Workers' City, Administration & Construction Logistics: Building the Greatest Engineering Project of the Ancient World
PURPOSE OF THIS VOLUME
This volume reconstructs the living human infrastructure that made the construction, operation, and long-term maintenance of the Giza Plateau possible.

Without this layer, the pyramids become static monuments.

With this layer, they become the visible result of one of history's most sophisticated state-organized engineering projects.

The objective is to demonstrate that the construction of the pyramids was fundamentally a triumph of logistics, administration, planning, specialization, and resource management.

Visitors should understand that the greatest achievement was not simply moving stone—it was coordinating an entire civilization.

PERFORMANCE STRATEGY
Thousands of simulated individuals are desirable but must remain performant.

Implement:
- distance-based simulation,
- behavior LOD,
- animation instancing,
- pooled navigation,
- asynchronous schedule evaluation,
- simplified AI for distant crowds,
- full behavior trees only within interaction range.
The world should feel densely populated while remaining efficient in a browser environment.

The simulation should replace common myths with evidence-based understanding through environmental observation rather than lengthy text panels.
THREE.JS IMPLEMENTATION
The Workers' City should be implemented as a modular simulation layer independent of the monument rendering pipeline.

Recommended architecture:
WorkersCity
├── DistrictManager
├── HousingSystem
├── ProfessionManager
├── NPCScheduler
├── EconomySystem
├── WorkshopSystem
├── HarborSystem
├── LogisticsManager
├── CrowdSimulation
├── AudioSystem
├── Metadata
└── EducationalOverlay

Key technical principles:
- Data-driven NPC definitions loaded from JSON or a database.
- Entity Component System (ECS) or equivalent composition model for scalability.
- Navigation meshes generated per district and streamed with terrain chunks.
- Hierarchical behavior simulation (full AI near the user, statistical simulation at long range).
- GPU instancing for repeated props (amphorae, baskets, tools, crates, benches).
- Animation state machines with shared skeletons for profession families.
- Event system capable of triggering scheduled activities (shift changes, deliveries, ceremonies).
- Persistent world state so construction progress, inventories, and NPC assignments can evolve over time.
